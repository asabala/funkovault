## Vision

Atlas Academy should not feel like a traditional FAQ page.

It should feel like a modern, interactive learning experience that helps collectors become more knowledgeable while making Atlas the go-to destination for both collection management and collector education.

This feature has the potential to become one of Atlas's signature experiences and further reinforce the project's philosophy:

**Simple. Scalable. Beautiful. Intelligent. Collector First.**
