# The Funko Vault Project Charter

## Vision

The Funko Vault Project exists to build the most trusted and enjoyable collector platform available.

Our goal is not to create the largest application.

Our goal is to create the application collectors never want to leave.

---

## Mission

Build software for collectors, by collectors.

Preserve collections.

Preserve memories.

Preserve stories.

Atlas exists to quietly empower collectors through intelligence while always keeping the collector in control.

---

## Core Principles

• The collector always comes first.

• Every collectible has a story.

• Every feature must solve a real problem.

• Architecture should outlive individual features.

• Trust is earned through accuracy.

• Community should encourage collecting, not competition.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Funko Vault begins as a personal collector application.

It grows into a complete collector platform.

Atlas OS grows into the intelligence layer that powers every collector experience.

The platform should be capable of evolving for years without losing sight of its original mission.

---

## The Standard

We are not building something merely good.

We are not building something merely great.

We are building something we are proud to put our names on.

Every sprint should leave the project stronger than it was before.

Every decision should make collecting more enjoyable.

Every line of code should move us toward that vision.
