# Funko Vault Design Bible

## Mission

**We build software for collectors, by collectors. Every feature should make collecting more enjoyable, more organized, and more informed. Atlas exists to empower the collector—not replace them.**

---

# The Three Core Pillars

## 1. Respect the Hunt

We never automate away the excitement of collecting.

The thrill of discovering a grail, finding a rare chase, or completing a set is what makes collecting special.

Funko Vault exists to support the hunt, not replace it.

Atlas helps collectors make informed decisions while preserving the excitement of discovery.

---

## 2. Data Integrity Above Everything

A Vault is only as valuable as the information inside it.

Every collectible deserves accurate information.

Every value.

Every variant.

Every sticker.

Every purchase.

Every owner.

Every collection.

Atlas should always favor trusted information over assumptions.

When uncertainty exists, Atlas should clearly communicate it rather than present guesses as facts.

Accuracy builds trust.

---

## 3. The Box Matters

Collectors understand that every collectible has a story.

Condition matters.

Stickers matter.

Variants matter.

Autographs matter.

Purchase history matters.

Display history matters.

Nothing is considered "extra information."

Every detail contributes to the story of a collectible.

Funko Vault exists to preserve that story.

---

## 4. Intelligence Should Be Invisible

Atlas should never compete with the collection.

Atlas should never interrupt.

Atlas should never overwhelm the collector.

Atlas quietly delivers the right information at the right time.

The collection is always the hero.

Atlas is the guide.

---

## Engineering Philosophy

Every architectural decision should answer one question:

**Will this still make sense when someone owns 10,000 collectibles instead of 100?**

If the answer is no, redesign it.

---

## Product Philosophy

Funko Vault is not an inventory app.

It is a digital Vault built for collectors.

The goal is not simply to track collectibles.

The goal is to preserve collections, organize knowledge, and help collectors make better decisions throughout their collecting journey.

---

## Atlas OS

Atlas OS is the Collector Intelligence Engine that powers Funko Vault.

It coordinates intelligence across the application while remaining invisible to the user.

Atlas OS should never replace the collector.

Atlas OS should amplify the collector.

---

## Development Philosophy

No single person—or single AI—has all the answers.

Funko Vault is built by evaluating ideas on their technical merit, regardless of where they originate.

Architecture, engineering, design, and user experience should always be challenged, refined, and improved.

The product comes first.

The collector comes first.

Everything else is secondary.
