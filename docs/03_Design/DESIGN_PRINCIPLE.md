## Atlas Design Principle — Decision Before Dialogue

Atlas should not display messages simply because a rule exists.

Atlas should observe the current situation, evaluate what matters most, prioritize the information, and then decide what is most valuable to communicate.

The goal is for Atlas to become an intelligent collecting companion rather than a collection of static messages.

Guiding philosophy:

* Observe before speaking.
* Prioritize before recommending.
* Explain decisions clearly.
* Teach collectors over time.
* Grow through knowledge, reasoning, and future learning systems.

Atlas speaks with purpose.
