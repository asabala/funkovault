# Atlas Intelligence Principles

> *Atlas is built to become the world's most trusted collection intelligence platform.*
>
> Every feature, engine, module, and AI capability should follow these principles.

---

# Principle 1 — Truth Over Assumption

Atlas never guesses when evidence is available.

If evidence is incomplete, Atlas communicates uncertainty instead of pretending certainty.

---

# Principle 2 — Evidence Before Decisions

Every recommendation must be backed by structured evidence.

Nothing is "because the code says so."

Everything is explainable.

---

# Principle 3 — Explain Every Recommendation

Atlas should always be able to answer:

> Why?

Every recommendation should expose the evidence that produced it.

---

# Principle 4 — Collector Always Wins

The collector owns the collection.

Automation never silently overwrites collector data.

Atlas recommends.

Collectors decide.

---

# Principle 5 — Trust Is Earned

Every piece of information has a source.

Examples:

• Collector
• Catalog
• Scanner
• CSV
• API
• Community
• Atlas Intelligence

Every source has a confidence level.

---

# Principle 6 — Build Once. Reuse Everywhere.

Atlas never solves the same problem twice.

Reusable engines are preferred over screen-specific code.

Reusable intelligence is preferred over collectible-specific logic.

---

# Principle 7 — Intelligence Is Modular

Every collectible category plugs into Atlas through modules.

Examples:

Funko

Pokémon

Hot Wheels

Sports Cards

Coins

Comics

Autographs

Future categories should require new modules—not platform rewrites.

---

# Principle 8 — The Scanner Is the Beginning

Scanning is not the product.

Scanning begins the intelligence process.

Barcode

↓

Identity

↓

Evidence

↓

Decision

↓

Recommendation

↓

Collector Approval

---

# Principle 9 — Atlas Learns From Knowledge

Atlas combines information from multiple trusted sources.

Examples:

Collector

CSV

Scraper

Catalog APIs

Marketplace APIs

Future knowledge providers

No single source defines truth.

Truth emerges from evidence.

---

# Principle 10 — AI Explains, Not Invents

Artificial Intelligence enhances reasoning.

It does not replace evidence.

Atlas AI should never fabricate certainty.

---

# Principle 11 — Every Decision Is Reproducible

Given the same evidence and policy,

Atlas should reach the same conclusion.

Deterministic reasoning builds trust.

---

# Principle 12 — Platform Before Product

Every new capability should strengthen Atlas itself.

Never build a feature that cannot become part of the platform.

---

# Principle 13 — Design for the Next Collector

Today's work should make tomorrow's collectible easier to support.

Architecture should outlive categories.

---

# Principle 14 — Respect the Collection

Every collectible represents someone's time, money, and memories.

Atlas treats every collection with the same level of care as its owner.

---

# Principle 15 — Atlas Exists to Help Collectors Make Better Decisions

Not faster decisions.

Not automated decisions.

Better decisions.
