# ADR-005 — Atlas Intelligence Architecture

**Status:** Approved

**Version:** 1.0

**Date:** July 29, 2026

---

# Purpose

Atlas Intelligence is the analytical brain of Atlas.

Its responsibility is to transform raw collector data into meaningful insights, recommendations, explanations, and guidance.

Atlas Intelligence never replaces the collector.

Atlas helps collectors make better decisions.

---

# Vision

Traditional collection software stores information.

Atlas understands information.

Atlas Intelligence continuously analyzes:

• Collections

• Collectibles

• Market History

• Timeline Events

• Catalog Data

• User Activity

to generate useful insights.

---

# Core Principle

Atlas never asks:

"What data do we have?"

Atlas asks:

"What does this data mean?"

---

# Mission

Help collectors answer one question.

"Should I buy this?"

Everything within Atlas Intelligence exists to help answer that question.

---

# Intelligence Philosophy

Atlas Intelligence assists.

Collectors decide.

Recommendations are suggestions.

The collector always remains in control.

Atlas never automatically changes collection data without user approval.

---

# Atlas AI

Atlas AI is the conversational interface.

Atlas Intelligence is the analytical engine.

Atlas AI explains the results generated by Atlas Intelligence.

Example

Atlas Intelligence

"This collectible increased 18%."

Atlas AI

"This collectible has increased steadily over the last six months and is now one of the fastest-growing Freddy Funkos in your collection."

---

# Intelligence Rules

Every intelligence engine must:

Have a single responsibility.

Be reusable.

Remain independent from UI.

Produce deterministic results whenever possible.

Expose clean APIs.

Avoid duplicate business logic.

Helpful

Trustworthy

Transparent

Explainable

Reusable

Fast

# Long-Term Vision

Atlas becomes more than a collection manager.

Atlas becomes a trusted collecting companion.

Over time Atlas learns the collection, understands collecting habits, identifies opportunities, and preserves the collector's journey.

Atlas should feel like collecting with an expert sitting beside you.
