# Atlas Decision Contract
### Version 1.0
### "Structured Conclusions Built from Findings"

---

# Purpose

A Decision represents the final conclusion produced by a Decision Engine.

Every Decision is supported by Findings.

Every Decision is explainable.

---

# Philosophy

Atlas never produces unexplained conclusions.

Reasoning is preserved.

Evidence is preserved.

Findings remain visible.

---
